# Lacanian warrant registry v0.1

This registry prevents theory from becoming atmosphere. It does not claim that Lacanian discourse analysis is a closed codebook. Each card states what a concept can distinguish here, what evidence it needs, and what it cannot establish.

Source keys refer to `sources.md`.

## Card: sequence and repair

- **Lineage:** conversation analysis bridge
- **Operational use:** locate how one turn constrains the next, how trouble becomes visible, and which repair changes subsequent uptake.
- **Question:** what response became relevant, and how did the next turns ratify, resist, recast, or repair it?
- **Required evidence:** ordered turns plus explicit or strongly inferable adjacency; repair claims require the trouble source, repair initiation or intervention, and downstream uptake.
- **Disallowed inference:** adjacency alone proves motive or causality.
- **Sources:** `CA-SYSTEMATICS-1974`, `CA-REPAIR-1977`.
- **Reuse status:** core.

## Card: enunciation and enunciated

- **Lineage:** Lacanian
- **Operational use:** distinguish the proposition stated from the speaking position enacted in stating it.
- **Question:** what does the utterance say, and what relation among speaker, addressee, knowledge, or authority does the act of saying institute?
- **Required evidence:** wording plus address and uptake. The posited speaking position must explain a consequential mismatch between literal content and relational operation.
- **Disallowed inference:** proof of unconscious intention, deception, or a stable hidden self.
- **Sources:** `LACAN-ECRITS`, `PARKER-2005`, `SWALES-2026`.
- **Reuse status:** core.

## Card: signifier and signifying chain

- **Lineage:** Lacanian
- **Operational use:** trace how a word, name, contrast, or formula acquires force through repetition, substitution, echo, and later reinterpretation.
- **Question:** how does this signifier alter what can be said or recognized in subsequent turns?
- **Required evidence:** at least two sequentially related uses or a single marked introduction with demonstrable downstream uptake. Track shifts in function, not only dictionary meaning.
- **Disallowed inference:** frequency by itself establishes importance; an analyst-selected keyword is necessarily organizing for participants.
- **Sources:** `LACAN-ECRITS`, `PARKER-2005`, `SWALES-2026`.
- **Reuse status:** core.

## Card: subject position and address

- **Lineage:** Lacanian with conversation-analysis bridge
- **Operational use:** describe the place from which a turn becomes sayable and the place it offers or imposes on its recipient.
- **Question:** who can know, correct, recognize, judge, witness, or require reassurance in this turn, and how does the addressee accept or resist that position?
- **Required evidence:** grammatical or explicit address cues, reply/relay structure, and uptake. Recurrence is required before claiming a durable interactional pattern.
- **Disallowed inference:** the position is a personality type, clinical identity, durable preference, or participant essence.
- **Sources:** `PARKER-2005`, `SWALES-2026`, `CA-SYSTEMATICS-1974`.
- **Reuse status:** core.

## Card: supposed knowledge

- **Lineage:** Lacanian
- **Operational use:** identify how the conversation locates authority or knowledge in a speaker, addressee, absent participant, system, or shared symbolic order.
- **Question:** whose recognition would settle the uncertainty, and what changes when that participant knows, fails to know, or refuses the assigned office?
- **Required evidence:** explicit attribution of knowledge or a repeated sequence in which turns solicit, defer to, challenge, or repair around the same knowing position.
- **Disallowed inference:** the participant privately believes the attribution, actually possesses the knowledge, or permanently occupies that position.
- **Sources:** `LACAN-ECRITS`, `PARKER-2005`, `SWALES-2026`.
- **Reuse status:** core.

## Card: organizing signifier

- **Lineage:** operational bridge; related to Lacanian anchoring and master-signifier questions
- **Operational use:** name a signifier that temporarily organizes several otherwise dispersed uncertainties or positions in the observed conversation.
- **Question:** does this signifier coordinate multiple later turns, redistribute positions, or make a previously diffuse conflict legible?
- **Required evidence:** recurrence across distinct speakers or phases; a demonstrable change in address, uptake, or repair around it; and at least one plausible alternative organizer considered.
- **Disallowed inference:** every salient or repeated word is a master signifier; the signifier has a universal meaning; the observed organization proves clinical structure.
- **Sources:** `LACAN-ECRITS`, `PARKER-2005`, `SWALES-2026`.
- **Reuse status:** core under the modest label “organizing signifier.” Use “master signifier” only when the stronger theoretical claim is explicitly argued and sourced.

## Deferred concepts

The four discourses, Imaginary/Symbolic/Real topology, objet petit a, fantasy, jouissance, sexuation, and clinical structures are not part of the default v0.1 protocol. They may be used only as an explicitly requested advanced extension with independent sourcing, an operational warrant, and prominent limits. They must never be inferred from a short conversation by pattern matching.
