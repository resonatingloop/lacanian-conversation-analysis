# Example 004: the borrowed voice

> Status: wholly synthetic conversation  
> Input shape: ordered Markdown with local event references, direct quotation, and declared relay  
> Participants: three humans  
> Completeness: complete fictional exchange from instruction through attribution repair

## Conversation

```text
m01 — nyla [human]
ion, tell rafi: “the west door stays open until the delivery arrives.”

m02 — ion [human, relaying m01 to rafi]
nyla said, “the west door stays open until the delivery arrives.” i disagree; it should close between entries because no one is stationed there.

m03 — rafi [human, replying to m02]
if you want the door open, you will need to staff it.

m04 — ion [human, replying to m03]
i do not want it open. those were nyla’s words; my recommendation was to close it between entries.

m05 — rafi [human, replying to m04]
then i answered the quoted instruction as though it were your recommendation.

m06 — nyla [human, replying to m05]
the delivery arrived early, so the door can close now. ion’s safety objection was separate and valid before the arrival.

m07 — rafi [human, replying to m06]
understood: temporary instruction from nyla; relay and objection from ion. i collapsed the two voices.

m08 — ion [human, replying to m07]
yes. record the instruction and the objection separately.
```
