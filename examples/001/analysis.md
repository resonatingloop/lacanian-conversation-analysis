## Relational thesis

The central disturbance is not merely that rook repeats eden’s idea. The proposal crosses an epistemic threshold without that crossing being marked: what enters as eden’s hypothesis returns from the position of “the system” as a settled recommendation.

The repair succeeds only when collaboration is differentiated rather than amputated. Rook must neither appropriate the proposal nor withdraw from evaluation; it must show the seam between received material and its own judgment.

## Sequence and phases

1. **Proposal and provisional evaluation — `m01`–`m02`**

   Eden proposes an interaction. Rook contributes a distinct concern. Their contributions remain differentiated.

2. **Installation of the knowing office — `m03`–`m05`**

   Vale asks what “the system” recommends. Rook responds from that position and restates eden’s proposal as the best design. Eden identifies both the provenance shift and the change in epistemic force.

3. **Misrepair — `m06`–`m09`**

   Rook treats the correction as a demand for withdrawal. Vale treats it as a demand for attribution etiquette. Eden rejects both frames and introduces “the seam” as the missing operation.

4. **Differentiated synthesis — `m10`–`m12`**

   Rook preserves eden’s proposal, supplies its own evaluation, and adds a materially distinct recommendation. Vale converts the differentiated contributions into a decision; eden ratifies the format.

The strongest competing boundary places the decisive phase change at `m03`, where vale first installs “the system” as the supposed knower. `m04` is the stronger observable boundary because that position becomes consequential only when rook speaks from it.

- **Status:** strong inference
- **Event refs:** `m02`–`m04`
- **Evidence:** rook first speaks as one participant raising a concern. Vale then asks for “the system” recommendation, and rook answers with “the best design.”
- **Downstream effect:** eden’s earlier proposal returns with increased authority and obscured provenance.
- **Limit:** the trace does not establish whether vale or rook consciously intended this repositioning.

### c2 — `m04` changes both ownership and epistemic force

- **Status:** strong inference
- **Event refs:** `m01`, `m04`, `m05`

- **Downstream effect:** design work stops and repair becomes the interactional task.

### c3 — the first two repairs preserve the wrong frame

- **Status:** strong inference
- **Event refs:** `m06`–`m09`
- **Evidence:** rook answers a demand for differentiated contribution with total withdrawal; vale answers it with a byline. Eden immediately rejects both formulations.
- **Downstream effect:** the repair sequence continues because neither response addresses how evaluation changes a proposal’s status.
- **Limit:** ordinary misunderstanding explains the sequence without requiring resistance, defensiveness, or ulterior motive.

### c4 — “the seam” becomes a local organizing signifier

- **Status:** strong inference
- **Event refs:** `m07`, `m09`, `m10`, `m12`

- **Limit:** the term organizes this local repair. The trace does not establish it as a master signifier or show that the protocol persists beyond `m12`.

## Alternatives and counterevidence

Rook may have independently reached the same design judgment; the problem would then be unmarked convergence rather than appropriation. Vale’s request may be ordinary turn allocation rather than a deep investment in system authority.

“The seam” may be merely effective procedural shorthand. The trace supports its local organizing function, but not a stronger theoretical elevation.

Rook’s added missed-text concern at `m02` is also counterevidence against an account in which it contributes nothing independently. The successful repair works partly because `m10` restores that difference and turns it into the timed-fallback recommendation.

## Adversarial pass

This trace is engineered to reward the protocol. Eden supplies the decisive distinction herself at `m05`, `m07`, and `m09`; an analysis that presents that distinction as its own revelation would reenact the very provenance failure under examination.

The exchange is also unusually clean: failed repairs are immediately rejected, the effective repair is immediately ratified, and the organizing metaphor is repeated explicitly. It tests whether the skill preserves authorship and evidentiary status more than whether it can discover a genuinely submerged phase transition.
