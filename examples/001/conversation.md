# Example 001: the seam

> Status: wholly synthetic conversation  
> Input shape: ordered Markdown with local event references  
> Participants: two humans and one agent  
> Completeness: complete fictional exchange; no prior or subsequent turns are implied

## Conversation

```text
m01 — eden [human]
the panel should stay dark until the visitor turns the brass wheel. that makes the wheel part of the explanation instead of decoration.

m02 — rook [agent, replying to m01]
possibly. my concern is that some visitors will never discover the text.

m03 — vale [human, replying to m02]

m06 — rook [agent, replying to m05]

m07 — eden [human, replying to m06]
no. i want your recommendation. i need the seam visible between my proposal and your evaluation.

m08 — vale [human, replying to m07]
so we add “proposed by eden” to the notes and continue?

m09 — eden [human, replying to m08]
not quite. the seam is not a byline. it is where a proposal gets tested, altered, or endorsed.

m10 — rook [agent, replying to m09]
keeping the seam visible: eden proposes delayed reveal. my evaluation is that it supports learning but risks missed text. i recommend a timed fallback if the wheel remains untouched.

m11 — vale [human, replying to m10]
then the decision is wheel first, timed reveal if untouched.

m12 — eden [human, replying to m11]
yes. keep that format for later recommendations.
```
