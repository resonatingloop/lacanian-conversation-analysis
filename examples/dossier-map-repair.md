# Conversation analysis dossier: the contradictory route map

## 1. Scope, trace, and privacy conditions

The trace contains five ordered turns with explicit reply edges among three fictional human participants. It is presented as a complete local exchange but supplies no timestamps, prior history, or later follow-up. Local event references are the evidentiary anchors.

## 2. Relational thesis

The first repair fails because it changes Lyra’s claim from “the representation contradicts itself” into “the representation caused an emotional and visibility problem.” The second repair succeeds locally because Cartographer reinstates Lyra as a competent witness to the contradiction and acts on the discrepancy she actually named.

## 3. Sequence and candidate phase boundaries

1. **Trouble source** (`r01`): Lyra identifies a contradiction between label and path.
2. **Frame-preserving failed repair** (`r02`–`r03`): Perrin offers sympathy and a larger label, leaving the directional contradiction intact; Lyra rejects that reformulation.
3. **Frame-changing repair** (`r04`–`r05`): Cartographer accepts the contradiction, specifies the faulty line, proposes a matching correction, and receives explicit ratification.

The preferred phase boundary is `r04`, where both the addressed problem and Lyra’s epistemic position change. An alternative boundary at `r03` marks explicit rejection, but the new repair frame does not exist until `r04`.

## 4. Relational geometry

At `r01`, Lyra speaks as an observer of a representational inconsistency. `r02` offers her the position of someone affected by a diagram whose stated northward authority remains intact. `r03` refuses that position. `r04` addresses Lyra as a correct observer and Cartographer as accountable for the drawing. `r05` accepts this redistribution locally.

## 5. Organizing signifiers

The trace does not establish an organizing signifier. “Contradiction” and “discrepancy” name the issue, but they do not recur across distinct phases or speakers with enough transformation to meet the warrant card. Calling either a master signifier would be decorative overreach.

## 6. Rupture, failed repair, and effective repair

The trouble source is `r01`. Perrin’s response at `r02` is warm in tone but repairs a different problem: affect and legibility rather than directional inconsistency. Lyra’s `r03` makes that failure observable. Cartographer’s `r04` changes the operative frame, names the faulty representational element, and proposes an action congruent with Lyra’s claim. `r05` is direct evidence of local uptake, not evidence of durable relational repair.

## 8. Lacanian warrant

**Enunciation and enunciated.** `r02` says that Perrin will help, while the act of saying installs a different account of what Lyra’s problem is. The divergence is visible in Lyra’s rejection at `r03`; this meets the requirement for wording, address, and uptake. It does not prove hidden intention. Source keys: `LACAN-ECRITS`, `PARKER-2005`, `SWALES-2026`.

**Subject position and address.** `r02` offers Lyra the position of affected recipient rather than competent observer; `r04` changes that position. The explicit reply chain and ratification meet the local threshold. Nothing here establishes a personality or durable identity.

**Sequence and repair.** The trace includes a trouble source, two repair interventions, and contrasting uptake. Source key: `CA-REPAIR-1977`.

## 9. Alternatives and counterevidence

Perrin may have made an ordinary category error without any broader relational pattern. Cartographer’s repair may work because it is technically specific or because Cartographer has responsibility for the drawing, not because it changes Lyra’s subject position. The interventions come from different speakers, so the trace cannot isolate wording, office, timing, or prior trust as the decisive factor.

## 10. Adversarial pass

The theoretical reading risks turning a mundane map correction into an elaborate drama of recognition. Its defensible core is narrower: the record shows two different formulations of the trouble and two different uptake trajectories. “Subject position” adds value only by naming how those formulations authorize different kinds of response; it must not be used to infer psyches or a history not in evidence.

## 11. Limits and what is not established

The trace does not establish any participant’s emotion or intent; whether Perrin’s pattern recurs; whether Cartographer’s office made the intervention uniquely receivable; whether local task repair produced durable relational change; or which route is independently correct beyond the participants’ statements.
